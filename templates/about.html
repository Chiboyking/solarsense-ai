How it works

1. Real weather inLive conditions from Open-Meteo, for any of Nigeria's 36 states
2. Zone-specific modelXGBoost trained per climate zone on real NASA POWER history
3. A forecast, not a guessPower prediction with a real confidence range, 1–24h ahead

What this honestly doesn't do yet

  • Not yet validated against real metered household output — no free dataset of that exists publicly for Nigeria, so accuracy is measured against real weather data with output derived from a documented physical model. Full detail on the Accuracy page.
  • Inverter comparison is a manual-entry demo, not a live connection to any specific hardware brand.
  • Runs as a single instance right now — not yet built for fleet/multi-site monitoring at company scale.

We'd rather tell you this up front than have you find it after a pilot starts.
