Privacy Policy

Last updated: this is a draft stub — not reviewed by a lawyer. Replace this notice once reviewed.

What we collect

Using the forecast dashboard requires no account and no personal information. If you choose to use the optional "My Data" feature, we store: the state/climate zone you're in, the power reading you enter, and a timestamp. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, device ID, or exact location — only the state-level location needed to route your reading to the right climate zone.

What we don't collect

No account creation, no cookies used for tracking or advertising, no third-party analytics or ad networks, no sale of data to any third party.

How data is used

Contributed readings are kept to improve future versions of the prediction model for the relevant climate zone. They are not displayed publicly against any individual, and there is no leaderboard or per-user tracking.

Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023

We aim to handle any data collected in line with the NDPA 2023. Because no personally identifiable information is collected through normal use of this app, most NDPA obligations around personal data processing do not currently apply — this will be reassessed if the data collected expands (for example, if user accounts are added later).
